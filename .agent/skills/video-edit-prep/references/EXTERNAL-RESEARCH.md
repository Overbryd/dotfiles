# External workflow research ledger

## latent-spaces/brag

Reviewed: 18 September 2026  
Source: `https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag/tree/main/skills/brag`

Read-only review covered `SKILL.md` and all linked workflow references: inspection, planning, composition handoff, audio, delivery, and tones. No scripts, packages, music, SFX, templates, or source code were installed or copied.

General lessons adapted into this skill:

- define hook, progression, one breather, and landing before effects;
- create energy with motion/cuts while preserving settled text reading time;
- treat beat/cue analysis as optional timing evidence, not authority;
- use few major cue locks and sparse motion-matched accents;
- separate creative/editorial decisions from motion implementation;
- validate a motion package with both its native checker and the project's own QC.

Deliberately not adopted:

- the 15–25 second product-ad shape as a universal default;
- bundled music/SFX or their licensing assumptions;
- product/UI-specific inspection rubrics;
- voice/TTS defaults;
- automatic HyperFrames installation, cloud use, or generic workflow routing;
- baking a poster into frame zero as a universal delivery requirement.

The resulting guidance was rewritten for source-footage editing, consent, local media handling, and longform/event recap needs.
